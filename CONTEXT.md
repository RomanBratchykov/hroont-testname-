# Croptato

Croptato presents agricultural analyses based on farmer information and external data, with a saved showcase and analyses requested during a visit.

## Language

**Farmer profile**:
The farmer-supplied information about a field and its management that forms the assumptions for an analysis.
_Avoid_: Verified field observations, when the information has only been reported by the farmer

**Showcase account**:
The single shared John account used by visitors to demonstrate Croptato. Accepted changes to its farmer profile are visible to subsequent visitors in Personal info and Soily's startup introduction.
_Avoid_: Private visitor profile

**Analysis**:
An assessment combining a farmer profile with retrieved external data to produce calculated results and their limitations.
_Avoid_: AI-generated statistics

**Published showcase**:
A saved analysis of predefined farmer inputs that visitors can inspect without requesting a new analysis.
_Avoid_: Synthetic dashboard fixtures, when referring to results calculated from retrieved data

**Live analysis**:
A new analysis requested by a visitor for specified inputs or a different assessment.
_Avoid_: Live measurements, real-time weather, when the underlying data is historical

**Demonstration fixture**:
Illustrative profile or dashboard content that has not been produced by a completed analysis.
_Avoid_: Calculated result, validated recommendation
